Privacy Policy for Natti_Claude_Connection

Effective date: 20 November 2026

1. Who we are

Natti_Claude_Connection (the "App") is a private, internal integration operated by Divyne London ("we", "us"). It connects our own QuickBooks Online company to the Claude AI assistant so that our staff can work with our accounting data. The App is not offered to other businesses or to the public.

2. Information the App accesses

  • Accounting data in our QuickBooks Online company, such as customers, vendors, invoices, bills, payments, items, accounts and financial reports.
  • Basic company details from QuickBooks Online, such as company name and ID.
  • OAuth tokens issued by Intuit, which let the App connect to QuickBooks Online.

3. How we use it

  • To answer our staff's questions about our own books and reports.
  • To create or update records (for example draft invoices) when a staff member asks, for that person to review.

Every action is started by a member of our team. We do not use QuickBooks data for advertising, and the App does not use QuickBooks data to train AI models.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or rent any data. Data the App retrieves is shown only to our authorized staff. To provide the AI features, the relevant data is processed by Anthropic, the provider of Claude, under Anthropic's commercial terms and privacy policy. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

5. Storage and security

The App runs on our own computer. Intuit OAuth tokens and app credentials are stored in a local configuration file on that computer and are not hard-coded or published. Connections to QuickBooks Online use encrypted HTTPS. Access is limited to our authorized staff.

6. Retention and deletion

The App does not keep its own copy of QuickBooks data beyond what is needed to complete a request. Disconnecting the App in QuickBooks Online revokes its access, and we delete the stored tokens when the App is no longer used.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
