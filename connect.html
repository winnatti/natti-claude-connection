Connect to QuickBooks Online

Natti_Claude_Connection is an internal tool for Divyne London staff.

To connect or reconnect: on the computer that runs the QuickBooks MCP server, run the server's authorization command. A browser window will open on the Intuit sign-in page. Sign in as the QuickBooks Online company admin and approve access for Divyne London's company.

If the connection expires or is disconnected, repeat the same steps to reconnect.
