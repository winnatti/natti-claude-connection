Natti_Claude_Connection

Natti_Claude_Connection is a private, internal integration used only by Divyne London staff. It lets our team use the Claude AI assistant (by Anthropic) to read and update our own QuickBooks Online company, through Intuit's open-source QuickBooks Online MCP server running on our own computer.

It is not offered to other businesses or the public.
